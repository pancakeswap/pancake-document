## The basics

FLASK is the delicious token that powers the Labswap ecosystem.

Earn FLASK from Farms and Syrup Pools, win it in the lottery, or [buy it on the exchange](../../trade/pancakeswap-exchange/), then explore its use cases:

* Stake it in [Syrup Pools](../../earn/cake-staking/syrup-pool/) to earn free tokens
* Use it in [Yield Farms](https://docs.pancakeswap.finance/products/yield-farming) to earn more FLASK
* Buy Lottery tickets in the [Labswap Lottery](../../play/lottery/)
* Participate in [IFO Token Sales](../../earn/ifo-initial-farm-offering/)
* Create your [Pancake Profile](../../earn/ifo-initial-farm-offering/nft-profile-system/) and mint NFTs
* [Vote on proposals](../../protocol/voting/) relating to the Labswap ecosystem

But that's not all -- there's much more on the horizon for FLASK!
