# 👉 如何进行跨链兑换？

1. 访问 [https://pancakeswap.finance/swap](https://pancakeswap.finance/swap)
2. 在代币选择器中选择**源链/目标链（From / To Chains）**和**代币（Tokens）**

<figure><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pancakeswap/pancake-document/en/.gitbook/assets/Screenshot%202025-06-10%20at%2011.54.24%E2%80%AFAM.png" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

<div><figure><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pancakeswap/pancake-document/en/.gitbook/assets/Screenshot%202025-06-10%20at%2010.40.16%E2%80%AFAM.png" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pancakeswap/pancake-document/en/.gitbook/assets/Screenshot%202025-06-10%20at%2011.55.04%E2%80%AFAM.png" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

3. 查看兑换详情：
   1. 源链/目标链、代币以及各自的金额
   2. 总费用（Total Fee）
   3. 价格影响（Price impact）
   4. 滑点容差（Slippage tolerance）

<figure><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pancakeswap/pancake-document/en/.gitbook/assets/Screenshot%202025-06-10%20at%2010.41.54%E2%80%AFAM.png" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4. 点击 **Swap** → 确认（Confirm）

<figure><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pancakeswap/pancake-document/en/.gitbook/assets/Screenshot%202025-06-10%20at%2010.42.17%E2%80%AFAM.png" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
