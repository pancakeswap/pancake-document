# ✖️ PancakeSwap X

PancakeSwap X introduces a whole new way to trade your favourite assets on PancakeSwap by aggregating a wide range of third-party liquidity and building straight into the popular PancakeSwap dApp. It enables more assets to trade, deeper liquidity, gas-less swaps, and much more:

* Better prices
* More tokens to swap
* Built-in MEV protection
* Gas-free swaps

{% hint style="success" %}
**PancakeSwap X is live on Arbitrum, Ethereum, BNB Chain, Base, and Robinhood Chain, supporting crypto token swaps. Real-world assets (RWAs) are additionally supported on BNB Chain, Ethereum, and Robinhood Chain.**
{% endhint %}
