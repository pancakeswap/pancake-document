# Senior Motion / Interaction Designer

### **General Information**

* Salary range: Competitive, negotiable
* Position: Full-time
* Location: Remote

### Role:

* Attention to Detail: You are meticulous and continuously refine product interaction designs through motion design improvements.
* Problem Solving: Take ownership of design challenges, viewing them as opportunities to achieve goals.

### Responsibilities:

* Create interaction design for product video; tutorials; and marketing collaterals, deliver great motion experiences for a diverse range of DeFi audiences.
* Craft interactive elements for seamless interactions; refine designs through motion graphics, 3D motion effects, interactive icons design etc.
* Ensure visual consistency across the product interaction UI elements and maintain an efficient design language for the design team.
* Cross-Team Collaboration: Work with product managers, engineers, and marketing teams to drive design initiatives and ensure a unified vision.
* Design Ownership: Take ownership of the design process, ensuring alignment with project goals. Stay ahead of industry trends by analyzing competitor products.

### Requirements

* Extremely user/audience/content-focused mindset.
* Communication Skills: Excellent verbal and written English skills.
* Design Tool Proficiency: Any tools that create motions: after effects; 3D tools; or even AI.
* Web3 Familiarity: Understanding of Web3 technologies and applications
* Industry Experience: Proven background in fintech or blockchain, demonstrated through a portfolio.
* Experience: 3-5+ years as a full-time Motion Designer.

### Cultural Fit:

* Intellectual Curiosity: Strong desire to learn and grow.
* Enthusiastic and Self-Motivated: Open-minded and proactive.
* Adaptability: Thrive in uncertainty and challenges; resilient and hands-on.

### Bonus Points:

* Experience using fintech apps or PancakeSwap products.
* Passionate about crypto and knowledgeable in Web 3.0.
* Experience working in distributed teams.
