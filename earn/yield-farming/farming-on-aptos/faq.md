If you are a project building on Aptos looking to incentivize the liquidity of your token, check out [this](broken-reference) guide and get in touch with us!

### Why there isn't an "Enable" button?

On Aptos, coins don't have to be enabled/approved for spending. So there is no need to enable contract spending on farms. Simply click the "deposit" button to start farming.

### Do I earn FLASK on natively Aptos?&#x20;

Yes, unlike crosschain farming, you will earn FLASK rewards natively on Aptos.&#x20;

With our multichain expansion and deployment on Aptos. FLASK is now a multichain token that exists both on BNB Smart Chain and Aptos.&#x20;

FLASK on Aptos is equal to FLASK on BNB Smart Chain and can always be bridged between two chains with a 1:1 ratio.&#x20;

Please note that there is only one FLASK. There are no different versions of FLASK between different chains. And the total supply of FLASK across all blockchains will be capped at 750M, according to our v2 tokenomic litepaper.

To bridge your FLASK to BNB Smart Chain, check out the [FLASK bridging guide](../../../welcome-to-pancakeswap/how-to-guides/get-started-aptos/cake-bridging-guide.md).
