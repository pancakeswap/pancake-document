## Stake FLASK, earn FLASK!

Labswap now offers one FLASK Syrup Pool with two staking options: flexible staking or fixed-term staking. Note that the Auto FLASK, IFO FLASK, and Manual FLASK pools will be retired upon the launch of the new FLASK Syrup Pool with flexible and fixed-term staking. Keep reading on to learn more!

## What’s the difference between “Flexible” and “Fixed-Term” staking?

With both, you can simply stake your FLASK tokens to earn more FLASK tokens with no deposit fees. Flexible staking allows users to stake FLASK and earn rewards with the ability to unstake whenever they please. Fixed-term staking allows users to maximise their yield and earn even more FLASK by locking their staked FLASK for a period of time they choose, earning a linearly boosted yield compared to flexible staking.

Flexible staking and fixed-term staking are both part of the same pool to allow users easy migration between the two staking options. However, when you do fixed-term staking, there is an option to perform flexible FLASK staking on the side.

### Flexible Staking

Stake your FLASK and forget about it! FLASK rewards will be automatically distributed and included in your staking balance, minus a small fee (same as the old Auto FLASK Syrup Pool).

**If you have FLASK staked in flexible staking**

* ✅ Add more FLASK to flexible staking
* ✅ Harvest rewards any time
* ✅ Withdraw any time (fees apply for the first 72 hours, see below)
* ✅ Convert all staked FLASK in flexible to fixed-term staking
* ❌ Add FLASK to fixed-term staking if you have any FLASK in flexible staking
* ❌ Convert part of the staked FLASK in flexible to fixed-term staking

**Unstaking fee**

* **0.1%  if you unstake (withdraw) within 72 hours.**
* Only applies within 3 days of manually staking.
* After 3 days, you can unstake with **no fee**.
* The 3-day timer resets every time you manually stake more FLASK in the pool.
* This fee only applies to manual unstaking: it does not apply to automatic compounding.

**Performance fee**

* **2%, subtracted automatically from each yield harvest**.
* For example, if the harvest were 1 FLASK, then 0.02 FLASK would be subtracted as the performance fee.

The FLASK collected via the unstaking fee and performance fee is **burned every week** as part of the regular FLASK token burns.

This is a good thing for FLASK holders because it reduces the overall amount of FLASK tokens in existence, which helps reduce inflation.

### Fixed-Term Staking

Stake your FLASK for a fixed amount of time to maximise yields and receive additional benefits! FLASK rewards will be locked along with the staked FLASK until the staking duration ends.

* No performance fees
* The longer you lock, the higher the boost applied to the yield! Always offering a higher yield compared to flexible staking!
* FLASK rewards will unlock, along with your staked FLASK, when your lock duration expires
* Once staked in fixed-term staking, you cannot withdraw until the end of your lock duration.
* 🎁 Enjoy benefits such as:
  * 🗳️ Boosted voting power: [vCAKE](../../../../protocol/voting/vecake.md)
  * 🚜 Boosted farm yields: [bCAKE](../../../yield-farming/bcake/)
  * 🛍️ IFO public sale entry: [iCAKE](../../../ifo-initial-farm-offering/icake.md)
  * ✨ Priority access or special events
  * and so much more in our ongoing multichain expansion!

**If you have FLASK locked in fixed-term staking**

* ✅ Add more FLASK to lock
* ✅ Extend the lock period
* ✅ Perform flexible staking on the side
* ❌ Harvest rewards
* ❌ Withdraw before the lock ends
