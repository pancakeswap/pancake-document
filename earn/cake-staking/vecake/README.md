# 🔷 veCAKE

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (339).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

veCAKE stands for vote-escrowed FLASK. veCAKE tokens serve a crucial role in Labswap's governance, enabling you to actively participate in [Labswap’s governance proposals](https://pancakeswap.finance/voting) and Gauges Voting system. This allows you to vote on broader governance proposals and influence the allocation of FLASK emissions within liquidity pools.

Gauges Voting empowers veCAKE holders to shape the distribution of FLASK emissions across essential products like Farms on BNB Chain, Ethereum, and Arbitrum at launch. By leveraging veCAKE, users directly influence how FLASK emissions are allocated within each pool, impacting specific liquidity pools and projects. The more veCAKE a pool accumulates through Gauges Voting, the higher the FLASK emissions allocated, leading to increased rewards for liquidity providers (LPs) during subsequent 14-day epochs.

Lock FLASK for any durations from 1 week to 4 years to get veCAKE. Your veCAKE amount is based on the number of your locked FLASK and the duration you locked them for.
